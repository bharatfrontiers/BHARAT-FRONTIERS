# BHARAT FRONTIERS — Images

This folder contains the image library for the BHARAT FRONTIERS publication.

Images are organized by their primary use or publication section.

## Folder Structure

### homepage/
Images used on the main BHARAT FRONTIERS homepage.

### articles/
Images associated with individual articles.

### science/
Images primarily associated with Science content.

### technology/
Images primarily associated with Technology content.

### policy/
Images primarily associated with Policy content.

### business/
Images primarily associated with Business content.

### research/
Images primarily associated with Research content.

### innovation/
Images primarily associated with Innovation content.

### digital-edition/
Images used in Digital Edition issues and related publication material.

## Image Management

Images may later be uploaded and managed through the BHARAT FRONTIERS Admin module.

The Admin module will manage editable media without changing the permanent website structure.

## Naming Convention

Use clear and descriptive filenames.

Examples:

`india-semiconductor-facility.jpg`

`ai-research-laboratory.jpg`

`space-mission-launch.jpg`

`quantum-computing-research.jpg`

Avoid filenames such as:

`image1.jpg`

`photo123.png`

`final-final2.jpg`

## Important

Article metadata and article text do not belong in this folder.

Article content belongs in:

`../Data/articles.json`

Article page structure belongs in:

`../Articles/`

The image library is separate from the publication's content data.
